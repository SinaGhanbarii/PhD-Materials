# CFD Simulation of Hydrogen Separation in Pd Hollow Fiber Membrane 

DOI 10.1515/cppm-2015-0064 Received December 22, 2015; revised December 27, 2015; accepted December 27, 2015 

Abstract: In this article, hydrogen adsorption from gas mixture involve nitrogen and hydrogen by palladium hollow fiber membrane investigated and two dimensional model proposed for hydrogen adsorption in this model. This model has been evaluated based on equations (momentum and mass transfer) in all three parts (shell, membrane and tube) for existing gaseous compounds with using finite element method. The results of simulation validated by experimental data of hydrogen adsorption by palladium hollow fiber membrane. Modeling predictions shows good agreement with experimental data at different operating conditions such as different gas flows, temperature, pressure and etc. the result of simulation shows hydrogen separation efficiency increases with increasing temperature and pressure and decreases with increasing inlet rate. Also with using this model better performance of hollow fiber membranes can be obtained. In fact, hollow fiber membrane can be designed at different conditions and for different rates that it can save cost of various tests. 

Keywords: hollow fiber membrane, numerical simulation, Knudsen diffusion, hydrogen separation.

Recently, many studies have been conducted on Production of cheap hydrogen as a fuel of fuel cells is one of the fields [1]. Membranes can be used for a wide range on separations. To obtain desired separation, different type of membrane is suggested. In the past, plate-and-frame modules were used But recently, spiral-wound and hollow fiber modules have attracted many interests as they offer more compact package [3]. Also their mass transfer areas are more and better controlled. So the main advantage of HFMCs is contact without dispersion of two phases. In addition, the velocities of both phases can be chosen independently, so that neither flooding nor unloading problems arise [4]. 

Some experimental studies and modeling on the gas separation is done by HFMCs. Pan [5] reports a mathematical model to predict the flow of two component gas through asymmetric membranes considering pressure drop in permeation part [5]. Chern et al. have developed a model to simulate the performance of two component gas separation by HFMCs in isothermal process. This model simultaneously investigates pressure change induced by diffusion and permeability dependent concentration by dissolution-diffusion method. No validation of experimental results was reported in their study [6]. Koros and Thundyil proposed a new method to solve mass transfer equation in HFMCs permeation process for radial cross-flow, counter current and co-current pattern at twocomponent system and analyzed it theoretically [7]. Feng et al. have examined HFMCs to separate air and produce nitrogen and hydrogen. Presented mathematical model at this paper have been developed for current and two component mixture. This model has good agreement with experimental results [8]. Wang et al. [9] studied separation of CO2 and CH4 mixture by HFMCs. They presented a method that obtains the actual separation performance by HFMCs for two component mixture based on experiments and simulations [9]. 

Pan proposed a mathematical method multicomponent permeation by asymmetric hollow fiber membranes. This model also considers permeated pressure change at the fibers. Experimental data for multicomponent material confirms the accuracy of this mathematical model [10]. Li et al. proposed a model to separate gas mixture

## 2 Theory 

Nitrogen and hydrogen gas mixture enter to hollow fiber membrane (HFM) shell under a fully developed laminar flow and HFM is estimated as the cylindrical segment based on the Happel free surface model. As a result, this membrane has three parts: tube, membrane and shell. Static two-dimensional model is used on the parts. Mixture enters shell at z = L while air enters through tube at z = 0. As a result of indirect contact, hydrogen gas in the shell penetrates membrane and then tube. 

Model have been achieved based on the following assumptions: 

- Nitrogen penetration is zero. 

- The change in concentration of hydrogen and partial pressure in the pipes is ignored 

- Because only hydrogen penetrates and the pressure of the pipe is equal to one bar, the change in concentration of hydrogen and partial pressure in the pipes are neglected. Module is at constant temperature and static state. 

- Flow is laminar and fully developed 

- Chemical reaction does not occur in the module.

## 5 Conclusion 

In this article, hydrogen adsorption from gas mixture involve nitrogen and hydrogen by palladium hollow fiber membrane investigated and two dimensional model proposed for hydrogen adsorption in this model. This model has been evaluated based on equations (momentum and mass transfer) in all three parts (shell, membrane and tube) for existing gaseous compounds with using finite element method. The results of simulation validated by experimental data of hydrogen adsorption by palladium hollow fiber membrane. Modeling predictions shows good agreement with experimental data at different operating conditions such as different gas flows, temperature, pressure and etc. the result of simulation shows hydrogen separation efficiency increases with increasing temperature and pressure and decreases with increasing inlet rate. Also with using this model better performance of hollow fibermembranes can be obtained. In fact, hollowfiber membrane can be designed at different conditions and for different rates that it can save cost of various tests.
